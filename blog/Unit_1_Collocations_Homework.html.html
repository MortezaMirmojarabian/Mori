IntermediateSelf-studyInstant feedback

Practise choosing natural word combinations, distinguishing collocations from compounds and idioms, and using the expressions in context. Complete the scored activities first, then try the speaking challenges aloud.

1. Choose the natural collocation

Choose the best option in each sentence.

2. Complete the collocations

Type the missing word or words. Capitalization and final punctuation do not matter.

3. Match the words

Choose the word that naturally completes each collocation.

4. Collocation, compound, or idiom?

Choose the correct category for each expression.

5. Choose the best response to each situation

Think about the situation before choosing. The target collocation is shown after you check your answers.

6. Correct the collocation

Rewrite each sentence using a natural collocation.

The checker accepts the key corrected expression; compare your full sentence with the model feedback.

7. Speaking challenge

Answer these questions aloud. Try to use the target collocation naturally, then open the sample answer to compare your language.

1. Tell me about a time you made an effort to achieve something difficult.

Target: make an effort

Sample answer

I made a real effort to improve my English pronunciation, so I practised listening and speaking every day.

2. What do you usually watch on TV, and how often do you watch it?

Target: watch TV

Sample answer

I don't watch TV very often, but I sometimes watch documentaries or the news in the evening.

3. Have you ever been bitterly disappointed by a result or an event?

Target: bitterly disappointed

Sample answer

I was bitterly disappointed when I didn't get a position I had prepared for, but the experience taught me to keep trying.

4. Do you prefer a quick meal or a substantial meal when you are busy? Why?

Targets: quick meal; substantial meal

Sample answer

When I'm busy, I sometimes have a quick meal, but I prefer a substantial meal when I have enough time.

5. What ancient monuments or historic places would you like to visit?

Target: ancient monuments

Sample answer

I'd love to visit the ancient monuments in Rome because I'm interested in history and architecture.
