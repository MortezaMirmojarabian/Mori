6. Speaking challenge

Answer aloud. Try to use the target collocations.

  1. Describe a time when you made a special effort to achieve something.
  2. Have you ever learned something the hard way? What happened?
  3. What makes a good impression in a job interview?
  4. Do people today have too many demands on their time?
  5. What piece of advice would you give someone learning English?
Show useful collocations and a model answer

Useful collocations: make a special effort; learn the hard way; make a good impression; make demands on; a piece of advice; make good progress.

Model: “When I first started learning English, I didn't make much progress because I memorized individual words. I learned the hard way that I needed to study phrases in context. My teacher gave me a useful piece of advice: write complete example sentences. I made a special effort to follow that advice, and gradually made good progress.”
